Questions

Is AYORA a home service?+

Yes. Our wellness professionals travel to your selected location.

What should I prepare?+

A clean, comfortable and private space is generally sufficient.

Can I request a specific massage?+

Yes. Select your preferred wellness service when making your booking, and our team will confirm availability.

Do you provide services for men and women?+

Yes. Male and female professionals can be scheduled separately, subject to availability, applicable UAE requirements and AYORA service policy.

Can I book for someone else?+

Yes. You can book an AYORA session as a gift or for a family member.
