# New AYORA Earthy Noir Concept

## What will be built
- Add a seventh, separate design inspired by Midnight Noir without modifying the existing Midnight Noir page.
- Preserve its dark, polished atmosphere while shifting the identity to deep forest, warm ivory, muted terracotta, and restrained brass.
- Add the new concept to the all-designs gallery and make it available at its own shareable URL.

## Design direction
- Use stronger, less predictable typography and a flatter editorial layout instead of rounded glass panels.
- Replace the vessel-led hero with authentic treatment-focused imagery: warm oil preparation, therapist hands, towels, and a subtle home setting.
- Keep brass to small details; use ivory or terracotta for the main booking action.
- Use the revised, concrete brand language: “Authentic Ayurvedic therapies, brought to your door across the UAE,” “We bring the ritual. You keep your space,” and clear appointment benefits.
- Keep the useful proof points, relabelled as `06 Therapies`, `04+ Emirates`, and `HOME We come to you`.
- Use `THERAPIES · THE AYORA RITUAL · ABOUT · FAQ` and make `BOOK A SESSION` the sole prominent action.

## Content and interactions
- Carry across AYORA’s services, four-step process, audiences, FAQ, UAE coverage, and contact content.
- Keep the working WhatsApp and call controls, styled specifically for this design.
- Include subtle scroll and image motion with reduced-motion support.

## Technical details
- Create a new standalone concept page and a dedicated route wrapper.
- Generate original treatment photography for this design and store it with the project assets.
- Add the new route to static publishing configuration and the gallery list.
- Verify desktop and mobile presentation, contact links, route loading, metadata, and the final preview build.
